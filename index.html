Fivefold

How to play

Guess the five-letter word in six tries.

  • Each guess must be a real five-letter word.
  • After each guess, the tiles change color to show how close you were.

Examples

BLAST

B is in the word and in the right spot.

PRIDE

I is in the word but in a different spot.

MOUNT

U is not in the word.

Hints

Stuck? Tap 💡 for up to 3 hints per word. The first reveals one letter in its correct spot. The next two each remove 5 wrong letters from the keyboard. You can also give up to see the word.

Modes

Daily: one new puzzle every day at midnight. This counts toward your stats and streak.

Practice: unlimited random words, just for fun.

Hints

Hints don't affect your stats. Giving up in Daily counts as a loss. Shared results show 💡 with the number of hints you used.
